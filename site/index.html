Data Set 1 of the Justice Department's Epstein release is 3,097 FBI search photographs, beginning at the Manhattan townhouse. These rooms hold what those photographs caught, in the order they were taken, moved onto the White House public tour: the fire extinguishers, the banker's boxes, the network switches, the fuel drums, the boxing gloves.

Every photograph with no legible text hangs as an empty gilded frame. The people are the one thing kept out. Each black figure stands for 1,000 redactions in the release.

Object counts come from OCR text matched against a list of everyday objects; the redaction total counts fragments only, not their contents. Data: kabasshouse/epstein-data (CC-BY-4.0).
