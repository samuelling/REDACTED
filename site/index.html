Data Set 1 of the Justice Department's Epstein release is 3,097 FBI search photographs, beginning at the Manhattan townhouse. This house holds what those photographs caught, in the order they were taken, moved onto the rooms of the White House public tour: the fire extinguishers, the banker's boxes, the network switches, the fuel drums, the boxing gloves.

You arrive on the North Lawn. Walk up the drive and in through the North Portico.

Every photograph with no legible text is an empty gilded frame. The people are the one thing kept out. Each black figure stands for 1,000 redactions in the release. Everything is arranged by what it is: the office holds the computers and phones, the gym the weights, the old East Wing the storage and the vehicles. Click anything to see where it came from. Sit at a computer to read every web address in the emails. Press Figure to drop the ground and see who and what is left.

WASD walk · mouse to look · Shift run · click to inspect · Esc release the mouse

Objects are matched from OCR text against a list of everyday things; the redaction total counts fragments only, never their contents.
